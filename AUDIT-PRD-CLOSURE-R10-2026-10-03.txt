ROOT CAUSE
The CI bridge is signed by a private CA. R9 mounted that CA and exported
CURL_CA_BUNDLE/SSL_CERT_FILE, but HQ production adapter code did not provide
an application-level CA bundle path to PHP/libcurl. delivery.php and
object_storage.php kept verification enabled yet never set CURLOPT_CAINFO.
Therefore private outbound TLS trust was not encoded in the adapter config.
